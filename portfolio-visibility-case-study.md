## Overview

The Crypto.com Exchange App's wallet tab hadn't been updated since 2021. As trading behaviours evolved and the product expanded, the tab struggled to keep up — users had no visibility into PnL, no easy access to their positions, and no sense of the full range of products available to them. We redesigned it from the ground up to give traders the clarity and confidence they needed to make better decisions.

---

## The Problem (Before State)

**A product that had outgrown its design**

The original wallet tab, built in 2021, served a simpler product at a simpler time. By the time we began this redesign, it was showing its age:

- Tab items were grouped inconsistently, with no clear hierarchy
- There was no dedicated space to highlight product offerings
- Key trading data was either missing or hard to find
- The overall structure was too basic to support the decisions traders were trying to make

Users weren't lacking the data — the product just wasn't surfacing it in a way that was useful.

## Goal

To give active traders a clearer, more actionable view of their portfolio — surfacing key data points like PnL and product performance while reducing friction in accessing positions and transaction history.

---

## Research

**Workshop insights that shaped our direction**

Through an internal workshop, the team aligned on four core pain points that had been consistently flagged. These insights became the foundation for every design decision that followed.

01. Insufficient data and visual representation of the portfolio's performance
02. Lack of visibility into stats such as PnL and daily changes
03. Absence of token-specific details, including token cost and average price
04. Inability to view PnL across all product features

---

## Constraints

With limited engineering resources, we prioritised solutions that reused existing data and components, presenting them in a more meaningful way rather than building net-new infrastructure. This constraint pushed us toward cleaner, more focused design decisions.

## Solution

We rebuilt the wallet tab around three core jobs traders needed to do: understand their portfolio at a glance, track performance over time, and navigate to any position or product quickly. The redesign consolidates PnL, product discovery, and transaction history into a single, cohesive experience.

### Portfolio overview with PnL

The tab now opens with a high-level snapshot of total portfolio value, daily change, and unrealised PnL across all products. Users can see what matters most before scrolling — and toggle between views depending on how much detail they want.

[Insert: wallet redesign — portfolio overview, total value, daily change, PnL at a glance]

### Positions and product discovery

Open positions from Spot, Derivatives, and Earn are now consolidated in a single scrollable list under the portfolio summary. A dedicated product discovery section — built using existing cross-sell infrastructure — gives users a direct path to products they haven't yet activated.

### Transaction history surfaced in-tab

Previously buried behind multiple taps, transaction history is now accessible directly from the wallet tab with filtering by product type. This closed one of the most commonly flagged gaps — users no longer need to leave the tab to understand what they've done.

## Lessons

**Design effectively within constraints**

**Constraints are a design tool**
The restrictions we faced — limited resources, regional variation, existing component library — pushed us to be more creative and deliberate. Most of our strongest solutions came from reframing what already existed rather than building something new.

**Raise complexity early**
I didn't anticipate the regional restrictions until we were already deep into the design process. It's a pattern I now actively work against — I advocate for surfacing technical and geo constraints as design requirements at the start of any project, not discoveries midway through.

**Redesigns are rarely linear**
This project involved more back-and-forth than almost anything I'd worked on before. Navigating competing constraints while still delivering something meaningful taught me how to stay anchored to the core user problem when everything else is shifting around you.

## Closing

A long-overdue update that gave traders the visibility they needed — and set the foundation for a full product overhaul.

The redesigned wallet tab launched in late 2024, consolidating portfolio performance, product discovery, and transaction history into a single, cohesive experience. While post-launch analytics were owned by the product team, internal feedback highlighted the improved clarity of the PnL section and the value of bringing positions into the wallet tab.

This project also laid the groundwork for a larger, ongoing redesign of the full Crypto.com Exchange App — informed by what we learned here about user needs, regional complexity, and the limitations of the existing design system.
